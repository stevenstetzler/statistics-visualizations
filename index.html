These are experimental learning materials generated with Claude code or another AI model. They are what I tried to get AI to teach me about, using a visualization to prove a point.

Statistics Visualizations

Interactive explanations of statistical ideas.

  • Exploring Model Fit Degrees of Freedom [tutorial, viz]

    Why does fitting a line to three points leave only one degree of freedom? A 3D picture of data as a single point, the model as a plane, and the residual as the direction left over, followed by how noise in the data becomes χ² for the residual and a covariance ellipse for the fitted parameters.

  • Estimating Center and Spread [viz]

    Mean and standard deviation, median and quantile-based estimators, sigma clipping, and inverse-variance weighting, each applied to a Gaussian sample salted with Cauchy outliers, with histograms of what each estimator sees and a repeated-trials comparison.
